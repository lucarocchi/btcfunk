Data Sources & Methodology

Where the numbers come from and how they are calculated.

On-Chain

Bitcoin Blockchain — Google BigQuery Public Dataset

All on-chain metrics (MVRV realized cap, SOPR, NVT transaction volume, HODL Waves, CDD, Puell Multiple, Active Addresses, Whale Transactions, Exchange Flows) are computed directly from bigquery-public-data.crypto_bitcoin — Google's full mirror of the Bitcoin blockchain updated daily. Every transaction, input, and output since the genesis block (January 2009) is available.

Price

BTC/USD Daily OHLC — Kraken API

Historical and live BTC/USD prices are sourced from the Kraken public REST API (pair XBTUSD, 1440-minute interval). Data is available from October 2013. Prices are cached locally in SQLite and updated daily. The live price for MVRV market cap uses the Kraken ticker endpoint.

Exchange Addresses

Dune Analytics Spellbook

The 761 labeled Bitcoin wallet addresses used in the Exchange Flow metric are sourced from the Dune Analytics Spellbook (MIT license). Coverage includes 50+ exchanges: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Bitfinex, KuCoin, Bitstamp, Bybit, and others.

Update Frequency

All metrics are cached for 24 hours. BigQuery public datasets typically reflect blockchain state with a 1–2 day lag. On-chain data queries are incremental — only new blocks since the last run are fetched, keeping costs near zero.

Metric Definitions

MVRV Ratio

Market Value / Realized Value. Realized Cap = sum of all UTXOs valued at the BTC price on the day they were last moved. MVRV = Market Cap / Realized Cap. Values below 1 mean the market is below aggregate holder cost basis (historically a buy zone); above 3.5 historically coincides with cycle tops.

NUPL — Net Unrealized Profit / Loss

Derived directly from MVRV: NUPL = 1 − 1/MVRV. Represents the aggregate unrealized gain or loss of all holders as a fraction of market cap. Below 0 = capitulation; above 0.75 = euphoria.

SOPR — Spent Output Profit Ratio

For each BTC spent on a given day: SOPR = Σ(btc × price_at_spend) / Σ(btc × price_at_creation). Above 1 = coins moved at a profit on average; below 1 = selling at a loss (capitulation signal).

NVT Ratio — Network Value to Transactions

NVT = Market Cap / 28d-MA(daily on-chain BTC volume). Price cancels in the computation — only supply and transaction count matter. Works like a P/E ratio: high NVT = overvalued relative to actual usage.

Puell Multiple

Puell = Daily miner revenue / 365d-MA(daily miner revenue). Miner revenue = coinbase transaction output value (block reward + fees). When miners earn far above average, they tend to sell more — creating downward pressure.

HODL Waves & RHODL

The full current UTXO set is queried from BigQuery and bucketed into 12 age bands by days since last move. RHODL Ratio = BTC in 1–2y band / BTC in 1d–1w band — measures conviction of long-term holders relative to recent buyers.

Supply in Profit

For each HODL age band, the midpoint acquisition date is estimated and the BTC price on that date is looked up from the Kraken price history. Bands where the historical price is below the current price are counted as "in profit".

CDD — Coin Days Destroyed

CDD = Σ(btc_moved × days_since_last_move) per day. Weights transactions by dormancy — a spike means old, long-idle coins are moving, often a distribution signal near tops.

MVRV Z-Score

Normalizes the MVRV signal: Z = (Market Cap − Realized Cap) / stdev(all historical Market Caps). The standard deviation is computed over all daily market caps since 2013, making the Z-Score comparable across cycles despite Bitcoin's growth in absolute size.

LTH / STH Supply & Realized Price

Long-Term and Short-Term Holder supplies are derived from HODL Wave band aggregates. LTH = bands older than 1 year; STH = bands younger than 1 year. Realized prices are estimated by weighting each band's BTC by the price at the midpoint acquisition date, approximated from the Kraken price history.

Stock-to-Flow (S2F)

SF = Circulating Supply / Annual Issuance. Annual issuance is the sum of the last 365 days of coinbase revenue (from puell_daily). Model price uses PlanB's approximation: 10^(2.66 × log₁₀(SF) − 1.59). Historical SF is computed using a sliding window over the full puell_daily history.

Transaction Count & Fees

Queried daily from bigquery-public-data.crypto_bitcoin.transactions, excluding coinbase transactions. Volume = SUM(output_value) in BTC; average fee = SUM(fee) / COUNT(*). Incrementally updated — only new days since the last cached row are fetched.

Hash Rate

Derived from the blocks table: hash_rate (H/s) = AVG(difficulty) × 2³² / 600, converted to EH/s by dividing by 10¹⁸. The 600-second denominator is the target block interval. A 14-day moving average smooths out variance from natural block time fluctuation.
